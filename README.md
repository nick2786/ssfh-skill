# 寿山福海 / Shoushan Fuhai

## 中文介绍

寿山福海是一家深耕养老服务领域的专业运营服务机构，长期围绕机构养老、社区养老、居家养老、康养运营管理及相关产业协同展开布局。品牌名称取意“寿比南山，福如东海”，强调以中国文化语境中的尊老、敬老、孝亲理念为底层价值观，致力于为长者提供更有尊严、更有温度、更具专业性的养老服务。

公司持续以高品质养老服务为核心，逐步形成了涵盖养老机构运营、服务体系建设、康养服务管理、人才培养与输出、项目咨询与管理赋能等在内的综合能力。相较于单一物业型养老机构，寿山福海更强调“运营能力、服务标准、品牌口碑和复制能力”的长期积累，努力通过标准化、专业化与体系化建设，提升项目的持续经营能力与服务品质。

在服务实践中，寿山福海注重将养老服务的专业性与中国式家庭关怀相结合，围绕长者生活照料、健康管理、精神慰藉、文化活动、家庭沟通等方面，逐步形成具有自身特色的服务方法与服务表达。公司在长期运营中，持续关注长者及家属最真实的需求，强调安全、照护、陪伴、尊严和生活质量的平衡，不把养老简单理解为“照看”，而是更注重持续性的生活支持与身心关怀。

在发展路径上，寿山福海并不满足于只做单一院区运营，而是逐步探索“品牌化、专业化、平台化、轻资产化”的发展方向。一方面坚持夯实存量项目的服务质量和经营质量，另一方面也持续积累面向外部项目的运营输出、管理赋能与合作拓展能力，努力形成可复制、可协同、可持续的养老产业运营服务体系。

从整体定位看，寿山福海希望建设成为一家以运营能力为基础、以资本效率为导向、以服务品质为核心竞争力的养老产业运营服务商。公司强调，养老行业的长期价值不仅体现在床位规模或物业体量上，更体现在通过专业运营持续提升项目经营价值、服务价值与资产价值的能力上。

## English Introduction

Shoushan Fuhai is a professional eldercare operator and service platform focused on senior living, community-based elderly care, home care support, wellness operations, and related service integration. The brand name draws from the Chinese expression “longevity as enduring as the southern mountains, blessings as vast as the eastern sea,” reflecting a cultural commitment to respect, care, and dignity for older adults.

The company has gradually developed capabilities across senior living operations, service system design, wellness management, staff training, operational consulting, and project management support. Rather than positioning itself simply as a property-based care provider, Shoushan Fuhai places greater emphasis on operational excellence, service standards, brand trust, and replicable management capability.

In practice, Shoushan Fuhai combines professional care delivery with a distinctly human-centered and family-oriented approach. Its service philosophy covers daily living support, health management, emotional care, cultural engagement, and family communication. The company seeks to provide not only care and supervision, but also dignity, companionship, safety, and quality of life for elderly residents.

In terms of growth strategy, Shoushan Fuhai is not limited to operating individual facilities. It is also developing toward a more branded, professional, platform-oriented, and light-asset operating model. The company aims to strengthen both the service quality and business quality of existing projects, while continuously building external management output, operational enablement, and partnership capabilities.

At a strategic level, Shoushan Fuhai aspires to become an eldercare operating service provider defined by strong operational capabilities, disciplined capital efficiency, and high-quality service delivery. The company believes that the long-term value of eldercare lies not only in physical capacity or real estate scale, but in the ability to continuously enhance operational value, service value, and asset value through professional management.
